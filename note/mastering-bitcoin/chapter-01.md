# 📘 Chapter 1 — Introduction: What is Bitcoin?

## The Big Picture (در یک خط)

Bitcoin = digital money + no central bank + everyone checks the books together.

Chapter 1 says **what** Bitcoin is. Chapter 2 shows **how** one payment really works, step by step.

---

## The Main Idea

Bitcoin is a digital money system. There is no bank, no government, no boss. The users themselves run the system together using computers.

## 4 Key Building Blocks

Bitcoin is made of 4 parts working together:

1. **A peer-to-peer network** → many computers talk to each other directly.
2. **The blockchain** → a public notebook of all payments.
3. **Consensus rules** → the rules every computer follows.
4. **Proof-of-work** → the way everyone agrees on the truth.

## Short History

- **2008**: A person called *Satoshi Nakamoto* writes a 9-page paper.
- **2009**: The network starts.
- **2011**: Satoshi disappears. Nobody knows the real name.

Bitcoin solved a big old problem: **double-spending** (spending the same digital coin twice).

## How Bitcoins Are Made

- New bitcoins come from **mining** (computers doing hard math).
- A new block is made about every **10 minutes**.
- Maximum total = **~21 million bitcoins** forever. No more.
- The reward gets cut in half every 4 years ("**halving**").

## Wallets — 4 Types

A wallet is the app you use. Like a browser for the internet.

| Type     | Where            | Safety       |
| -------- | ---------------- | ------------ |
| Desktop  | Computer         | Medium       |
| Mobile   | Phone            | Common, easy |
| Web      | Browser          | Less safe    |
| Hardware | Small USB device | Most safe    |

## The Golden Rule (most important quote)

> "Your keys, your coins. Not your keys, not your coins."

This means: if someone else holds the **private key** (a secret number), the money is not really yours.

## The Small Story (Alice gets her first bitcoin)

1. Alice is new. She meets her friend Joe at a party.
2. Joe shows her Bitcoin.
3. Alice installs a mobile wallet. The wallet makes a **recovery code** (12 words she must save).
4. The wallet also makes a **Bitcoin address** (like an email for money).
5. She shows the address as a **QR code**.
6. Joe sends her **0.001 BTC** (= 100,000 satoshis). She pays him cash.
7. First the payment is **"Unconfirmed"**.
8. After ~10 minutes, a miner puts it in a block → it is now **"Confirmed"**.

🎉 Alice has her first bitcoin.
